# ACE Stores — store surfaces (home, catalog, product, checkout, library)

## Scope & visitor mode

Five surfaces of one store: home (Persuade), catalog + product + checkout + library (Operate). Desktop-first web; mobile designed in.

## Audience, job, action, proof, constraints

Video editors and music producers arrive mid-session needing a pack. Job: audition → buy (one price per pack) → download in minutes. Proof is the product itself: authored cover art, playable demos, contents and formats as facts. Constraints: 10–30 first-party packs; partial demo coverage is real (cover-only products get no player); English-first, Arabic-ready (logical properties); payment provider undecided (abstracted step); prices/media are clearly-labeled synthetic placeholders until real inventory.

## Chosen direction & memorable moment

**The Dark Creator Storefront — user-pinned canon.** The user chose the category-standard path over the previous Signal Print world, naming the craft bar: reasonstudios.com, signaturesounds.org, boomlibrary.com. Memorable moment: the massive uppercase claim hero with the featured pack block — Reason's grammar — and hover-play cover cards across the catalog.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The dark creator storefront, pinned by the user against Reason Studios, Signature Sounds, and BOOM Library — cover-art-led, massive bold type, one confident accent — executed at full category fidelity, without irony.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black neutral ground (#0b0c10), raised panels (#131519/#1a1d23), white ink (#f5f5f7), a single violet accent (#8b5cf6) for live/active/CTA-support, white-fill primary buttons; Inter Tight 800 display, Inter body; authored duotone cover art per pack; soft 14px card radii; real shadows, no glow clichés.

STORY: A creator lands on the claim and the featured pack, browses covers through two category tiles, hover-plays demos on cards, buys at one price, and downloads instantly.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Announcement strip, then a massive uppercase display claim (three staggered lines), beside the featured pack block: large cover art, name, price, play, add-to-cart — the Reason hero grammar carrying our product.

FORM: User-pinned canon (standing exit taken); craft bar = the three named sites; code-led build.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

## Unresolved decisions

Payment provider (simulated step until chosen); real prices, license wording, and real cover art/media (synthetic, labeled); account backend (session-local for this build).
